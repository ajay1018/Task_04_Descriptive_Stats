# 📊 Syracuse Basketball Analytics – Actionable Report

## 1. Objective
Translate raw player statistics and LLM analysis into **actionable insights** for coaching staff and decision makers.

---

## 2. Key Findings

- **Top Performers**
  - Judah Mintz leads with 602 points and an 18.8 PPG average.
  - Maliq Brown dominates rebounds (230 total, 7.19 per game).
  - Copeland provides balanced impact (597 impact score with 89 assists).

- **Efficiency Insights**
  - Brown (FG% = 69.8%) is the most efficient shooter.
  - Hima has the widest FG% vs. 3PT% gap (0.667 vs 0.000).
  - McLeod is a strong rim protector with 27 blocks in 14 games.

- **Bench vs. Starters**
  - Bench contributes ~25% of total points but excels in rebounds and defensive stats.
  - Starter heavy rotation shows fatigue risk—Mintz and Starling both logged 1,000+ minutes.

---

## 3. Actionable Recommendations

1. **Rebounding Focus**  
   Increase Brown’s minutes in close games; leverage his 7.19 rebounds/game to dominate boards.  

2. **Rotation Adjustments**  
   Reduce load on Mintz/Starling by expanding bench rotations (Taylor & Copeland).  

3. **Shooting Development**  
   - Improve 3PT shooting for high-volume players like Mintz (28.2%) to diversify offense.  
   - Continue maximizing Brown’s FG% by running more inside sets.  

4. **Defensive Strategy**  
   - Use McLeod and Hima situationally for rim protection.  
   - Capitalize on Brown and Copeland’s steals to fuel transition plays.
